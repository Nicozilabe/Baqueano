Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SSHM ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. SSHM is a command-line SSH manager distributed as an open-source project at github.com/Nicozilabe/sshm. It runs entirely on your own machine.

In short: SSHM does not operate any servers, does not collect telemetry or analytics, and does not send your data to us or to any third party. Any data you create stays on your device. If you enable the optional Google Drive sync, your data is stored only in your own Google Drive account.

1. Data stored on your device

SSHM works with your standard OpenSSH configuration files. Depending on how you use it, the following is stored locally on your computer, under your user account:

  • Your SSH configuration (for example ~/.ssh/config or a custom file you choose), which you manage through the app;
  • Connection history (last-connected timestamps) and port-forwarding history;
  • Automatic backups of your SSH configuration files made before changes;
  • An application settings file (config.json);
  • If you use Google Drive sync, an OAuth token used to access your Drive (see section 3), stored locally with restricted file permissions.

This data never leaves your device except as described in sections 3 and 5 below.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to any of your data. SSHM has no backend service, no account system, and no analytics or tracking. We cannot see your hosts, your configurations, your usage, or your Google account.

3. Google Drive sync (optional)

SSHM offers an optional feature to synchronize your SSH host list across machines using your own Google Drive. This feature is off unless you explicitly authorize it with the sshm drive login command.

Scope used. When you enable this feature, SSHM requests a single Google OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — "View and manage the app's own configuration data in your Google Drive".

This scope grants access only to a private, application-specific data folder (appDataFolder) that belongs exclusively to SSHM within your Drive. It does not grant access to any of your other files, folders, documents, or metadata.

What is stored in Google Drive. Only the host definitions derived from your SSH configuration (for example host names, hostnames, users, ports, identity-file paths, jump hosts and options) are stored in that folder so they can be synced to your other machines. Private keys and any file contents other than your host definitions are never uploaded.

How Google user data is used. Data accessed through Google APIs is used solely to provide and improve the sync feature you requested (uploading, downloading, and merging your host list). It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and transmission. The data is stored in your own Google Drive account under Google's terms, and is transmitted directly between your machine and Google's servers. It does not pass through, and is not stored on, any server operated by us.

Revoking access. You can revoke SSHM's access at any time from your Google Account security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions), and you can delete the local token file to remove the authorization from your device.

4. Limited Use disclosure

SSHM's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Update check

On startup, SSHM may check whether a newer version is available by querying the public GitHub Releases API. This request contains no personal data and is made directly from your machine to GitHub. You can disable it at any time with the --no-update-check flag or the check_for_updates setting in config.json.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data with third parties. We do not use your data for advertising or to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. You remain in full control of all data created by SSHM, which lives on your device and, if you enable Drive sync, in your own Google Drive account. You can delete local data by removing the relevant files, and Drive data by deleting the app's data or revoking access as described above.

8. Children's privacy

SSHM is a developer tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and committing the revised page to the project repository. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or SSHM's data practices, open an issue at github.com/Nicozilabe/sshm/issues.
